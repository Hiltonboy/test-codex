# Source Priority for Life Sciences 30-Day Briefing

## Tier 1 (Preferred)
- Government regulators and agencies (FDA, EMA, MHRA, Treasury/CFIUS, DOJ/FTC/SEC, EC).
- Company investor relations press releases / 8-K / official newsroom disclosures.
- Official court or arbitration publications.

## Tier 2 (Use with Tier 1 corroboration)
- Top-tier law firm client alerts and legal updates.
- Exchange announcements.

## Tier 3 (Context only)
- Financial media and industry news sites.
- Use only to discover leads; verify with Tier 1 or Tier 2 before citing as fact.

## Validation Checklist
- Include exact publication date for each source.
- Confirm URL is publicly reachable.
- Ensure the summarized claim matches the source language.
- Mark forward-looking statements as company guidance, not confirmed outcomes.
